    Hook up your synths

    1. In macOS System Settings → Sound → Output, pick BlackHole 2ch. Pandemonium, Pipsqueak and anything else playing in the browser now flows into BlackHole.
    2. Here, press power and allow audio input. Set Input device to BlackHole 2ch.
    3. Set Output device to your Apollo or speakers. This step matters: if the pedalboard also plays into BlackHole, it feeds itself.
    4. Open your synth in another tab and play. Guitar works too: set Input device to the Apollo instead.

    Use Chrome, and open the board from GitHub Pages or http://localhost. Opened straight from your drive, Chrome won't let it pick an output device. Echo cancellation and noise suppression are switched off so the browser doesn't scrub your tone.
